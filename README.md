# AI Restaurant Operations Simulator (Idea / Seeking Developers)

## The problem
Restaurant sims used in college hospitality courses feel scripted and flat.
Students learn to game the sim instead of learning to run a restaurant.

## The idea
An AI-driven restaurant sim where the world reacts like a real one:
- **Local map** (like Google Maps): neighborhoods, offices, shopping, foot and
  vehicle traffic, and competitors with their own ratings and menus.
- **AI guests and staff** who react to prices, service, quality, and marketing,
  within realistic limits.
- **Real problems to solve**: dead lunches or slow weekends that the right
  move (for example, targeted family or couples ads) can fix.
- **Difficulty that scales** from first-term students up to BA level.
- **Real numbers**: food cost, prime cost, labor, RevPASH, and true-usage
  inventory.

## Realism features
- **Vendors**: price swings, late or short deliveries, out-of-stock items,
  and produce yield loss (for example, chili stemming).
- **Crew**: call-outs, turnover, training time, and new cooks who cut corners.
- **Health inspections**: surprise visits where sanitation habits drive the score.
- **Equipment breakdowns**: a walk-in or fryer dying mid-rush, with repair costs.
- **Online reviews**: ratings that rise and fall with food quality and wait times.
- **Seasons and local events**: weather, holidays, paydays, and, near military
  bases, troop rotations and exercises.
- **Waste and theft**: spoilage, over-portioning, and pilferage that show up in
  true-usage food cost.
- **Fixed costs**: rent, utilities, and permits that hit whether you're busy or not.
